# Sahayak AI – Intelligent Emergency Call Assistant

## Overview
Sahayak AI is a smart emergency support system built to help helpline operators handle distress calls more effectively. In emergency situations, callers are often scared, stressed, or unable to clearly explain what is happening. This can make it difficult for operators to quickly understand the situation and take the right action. Sahayak AI is designed to reduce this gap by helping operators understand callers faster and respond more confidently.
The system listens to live calls and instantly converts speech into text. It then analyzes the conversation to identify important details such as the type of emergency, possible location clues, and the emotional condition of the caller. By understanding whether a caller sounds panicked, stressed, or calm, the system can estimate how urgent the situation is and assist operators in making quicker decisions.
Sahayak AI is not designed to replace human operators. Instead, it works like a reliable assistant that supports them during high-pressure situations where every second matters.
## Problem Statement
Emergency helpline services receive a huge number of calls every day. Many callers are unable to explain their situation properly because they are frightened, confused, injured, or under stress. In some cases, people switch between languages like Hindi and English or speak unclearly due to panic.
Because of this, operators may face challenges such as:
- Understanding the situation quickly
- Identifying the urgency level
- Extracting important information from the conversation
- Responding effectively during critical moments
These delays can affect emergency response time and decision-making when fast action is most important.

## How It Works
1. The system receives live emergency call audio.
2. Speech is converted into text instantly.
3. AI analyzes the conversation to understand the situation.
4. Emotion detection identifies the caller’s stress level.
5. Important details like emergency type and location clues are extracted.
6. A priority engine estimates the urgency level.
7. Suggested actions are shown to the operator in real time.
